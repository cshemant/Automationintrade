How the automation works

Exchange filing → five-factor scoring → evidence checks → searchable timeline

The daily Python generator downloads recent announcements, extracts company and filing information, classifies the business trigger, creates a concise non-recommendation summary, merges upcoming corporate actions and preserves recent history when the source is temporarily unavailable.

1. DiscoverFind important filings without reading every compliance announcement.
2. UnderstandSee why a development may matter and which risk should be verified.
3. VerifyOpen the original exchange filing before making any financial decision.

Educational information only. Materiality scores show business importance, evidence, magnitude, market confirmation and recency—not expected share-price direction and not a buy/sell recommendation. Read the full methodology.
